Sources and interpretation

Data from the HRM LakeWatchers monitoring results and station layer. This edition includes municipal deep-water stations with readings on at least two dates. Separate basins retain their station names. Depths are shown as metres below the surface. Every marker is a recorded reading at one depth on one visit. A blank represents an unreported measurement. Repeated readings remain visible in the table. These profiles describe sampled water at the station. They do not establish swimming conditions.

Compare editions and coverage in HRM LakeWatchers on DataStream. The snapshot uses the municipal ArcGIS source and can cover different dates. HRM Open Data Licence.
